yOdin

A university's dean's office agreed this project with us directly.

In dev

What they came with

International students arrive somewhere new and end up inside a group from their own country within about a week. It is the easiest thing to do, and it is the thing they later say they regret.

The project was agreed directly with the university's dean's office — discussed with an institution, not pitched at one.

What we built

A platform that matches students by how they actually behave — what they join, when they turn up, what they keep coming back to — rather than by which passport they hold.

Mobile first, because that is the only screen a student uses for this kind of thing.

What changed

In active development, and expanding beyond the university it started at.

This is the one project on this site you cannot open yet. It is here because how it started says something the finished ones cannot: an institution agreed to build it with us.
